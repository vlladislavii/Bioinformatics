TASK1
Gel electrophoresis is an analysis method implemented in all disciplines of life sciences. The result of gel electrophoresis indicates the relative sizes of fragments, which is useful for restriction mapping and analyzing PCR fragments.
1. Take an arbitrary DNA sequence from NCBI (National Center for Biotechnology) between 1000 and 3000 nucleotides (letters).
2. Take 10 random samples from this sequence, between 100 and 3000 bases.
3. Store these samples in an array.
4. Simulate the migration of these DNA segments on the electrophoresis gel, based on their molecular weights – however, their length should be sufficient for this exercise (draw a visual representation).
Note: Short DNA fragments meet small friction forces and travel faster through the electrophoresis gel. Long DNA fragments exhibit a high friction force and travel slowly through the electrophoresis gel.

TASK2
Take an arbitrary DNA sequence from NCBI, between 1000 and 3000 nucleotides (letters). Implement a software application that detects repetitions between 6 and 10 letters long in the DNA sequence.
These repetitions must be produced by a brute-force engine, and they must be combinations of between 3 and 6 letters.
